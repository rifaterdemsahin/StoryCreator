# Script Comparison: Original vs. Rewritten

This table highlights key differences between the original script and the rewritten version, demonstrating the shift in tone, stakes, and analogies.

| Section | Element | Original Script | Rewritten Script | Why the Change? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Course Intro** | **Hook** | "Your machine learning model just won the accuracy lottery... But here's the uncomfortable truth—accuracy and security are not the same thing." | "Imagine a radiologist using an AI assistant to screen for lung cancer... Suddenly, that 99% accurate model flags a healthy lung as malignant... This isn't a glitch. It's an attack." | **Higher Stakes:** Moved from abstract "accuracy lottery" to a life-or-death medical scenario to create immediate urgency. |
| **Module 1: Video 1** | **Hook** | "Imagine you're a security professional at a leading autonomous vehicle company... It's a stop sign with subtle stickers... Your system says, 'Speed limit: 45 miles per hour.'" | "You are the lead engineer for a self-driving truck fleet... on a foggy highway... The truck doesn't brake. It accelerates. This isn't a hypothetical nightmare—it’s a demonstrated vulnerability." | **Visceral Impact:** Added "foggy highway" and "truck" context to make the danger feel more physical and immediate. |
| **Module 2: Video 1** | **Analogy** | "Boxing trainers don't prepare champions by avoiding sparring partners. They prepare them by sparring against strong opponents repeatedly." | "Think about how a vaccine works... You introduce a weakened version of the virus to teach the immune system how to fight. Adversarial training is exactly that—a vaccine for your AI." | **Relatability:** "Vaccine" is a more universally understood concept of "protection through exposure" than boxing. |
| **Module 2: Video 2** | **Analogy** | "Think of input sanitization as airport security for your AI system... we preprocess everyone's luggage in ways that eliminate most threats." | "Imagine you run a high-security building... You also put a metal detector at the door. If something beeps, it doesn't get in. Input sanitization is that metal detector." | **Clarity:** "Metal detector" is a simpler, more direct mechanical analogy for a filter/sanitizer. |
| **Module 3: Video 1** | **Concept** | "Companies like Microsoft, Google, and OpenAI employ security professionals... They're called 'red teams.' ... They think like attackers." | "Imagine you built a vault. How do you know it's secure? You don't just look at the blueprints. You hire a master thief to try and break in." | **Storytelling:** "Master thief" vs "Vault" frames the concept in a classic heist narrative, making it more engaging. |
| **Module 3: Video 2** | **Hook** | "If you can't measure it, you can't manage it. In security, measurement is hard... We can't say 'my model is 100% secure.'" | "You wouldn't fly a plane without an altimeter... Yet, we deploy AI models every day without a 'Security Meter'. We know they are 99% accurate, but we have no idea how robust they are." | **Visual Metaphor:** "Flying blind" (no altimeter) is a stronger image of negligence than the abstract "measurement is hard". |

## Summary of Tone Shift

*   **Original:** Informative, Textbook, "Here is the information."
*   **Rewritten:** Narrative, Mentor-led, "Here is why this matters and how it affects you."
